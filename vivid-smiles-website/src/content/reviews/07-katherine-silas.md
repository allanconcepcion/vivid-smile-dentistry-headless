I have pretty severe dental anxiety and have had quite a few bad experiences in the past. The team at Vivid Smiles is so compassionate and understanding. They never make me feel rushed or judged, and they always explain exactly what they're doing before they do it. Dr. Richardson and his entire staff have made me feel genuinely comfortable and safe. I actually look forward to my appointments now, which is something I never thought I'd say!
